1. Why Consistency Beats Intensity in Fitness

Many individuals burn out because they attempt extreme workouts right out of the gate. Sustainable fitness is built on manageable daily activity—such as a 30-minute brisk walk or consistent stretching routine.

Regular movement boosts cardiovascular endurance, keeps joints lubricated, and improves overall mood regulation without overloading your nervous system.

2. Simple Stretching Exercises to Fix Posture

Hours spent sitting at desks or looking at screens can cause tight hip flexors and rounded shoulders. Implementing micro-stretches and mobility drills throughout the day restores spinal alignment.

Focus on doorway chest stretches, thoracic spine extensions, and neck relief movements to reverse the physical impact of prolonged sitting.
