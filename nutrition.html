1. The 10 Best Antioxidant-Rich Superfoods

Antioxidants are molecules that fight free radicals in your body. Free radicals are compounds that can cause harm if their levels become too high, linked to multiple illnesses including diabetes, heart disease, and cancer.

Incorporating nutrient-dense foods like dark leafy greens, blueberries, walnuts, and green tea provides your body with the cellular defense it needs to maintain long-term energy and radiant skin health.

2. How to Meal Prep Healthy Balanced Plates

Meal prepping takes the guesswork out of daily nutrition. By dedicating a couple of hours each weekend to prep foundational ingredients—like roasted vegetables, complex grains, and lean proteins—you prevent impulsive dietary choices.

Focus your portions around 50% fiber-rich vegetables, 25% quality clean protein, and 25% unrefined complex carbohydrates for an optimal metabolic balance.
